Help & options
Video not showing up?

Reload this page, press play and look again. Only videos the TV can fetch directly can be sent.

Connection and address

If the helper is disconnected, the address box cannot find your TV. Click Connect helper first.

Paste an address, then press Find TVs or Enter.

Other AirPlay TVs may appear, but playback compatibility is unverified. Audio-only speakers are not listed.

Ending the helper session closes its connection; it does not confirm that the TV stopped. Use the physical remote if playback continues. TV pause/resume are unavailable.

TV firewall permission

This checks saved UFW settings, not live packet delivery. Other firewall tools need manual review. A permission cannot fix unsupported video or TV compatibility.

The system asks for administrator approval; you can cancel. If this popup closes, approve on the desktop, then reopen PearPlay to see the result. PearPlay never turns the firewall off.

About website access

Access is granted only when you click. This version needs all-sites access to enable Find videos. A per-site grant alone does not enable discovery yet.
