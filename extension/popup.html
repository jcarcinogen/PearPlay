4. Choose an Apple TV

PearPlay looks on your network. You should not need to type an address. If the helper is not connected, Find Apple TVs and the address box cannot find your Apple TV — click Connect helper first.

TV not listed? Paste its address, then press Find Apple TVs (or Enter)

5. Send it

Ending the helper session closes its connection; it does not confirm that the TV stopped. Use the physical remote if playback continues. TV pause/resume are unavailable in this prototype.

After it is playing on the TV

Helper state is not visual or audible confirmation. Local playback is never automatically paused.
