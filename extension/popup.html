PearPlay

Only the enabled tab is observed, for 10 minutes. URLs stay in memory, not storage or logs. No main-stream guessing; candidates may be ads, segments, or unsupported DRM streams.

Current-site access does not cover other-origin frames or CDNs. Optional all-sites access allows these, but collection still only runs in enabled tabs. Permission grants do not recover earlier requests: reload, play the video, then rescan. Browser settings can revoke grants.

Blob URLs are excluded. A network candidate is not linked to a local video; local pause is only available for a video-element candidate.

Ending the helper session closes its connection; it does not confirm that the TV stopped. Use the physical remote if playback continues. TV pause/resume are unavailable in this prototype.

Helper state is not visual or audible confirmation. Local playback is never automatically paused.

Helper and CLI pairing must be installed separately. No cookies or credentials are forwarded. No media proxy or transcoding.
